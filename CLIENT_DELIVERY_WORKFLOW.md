# Client Delivery Workflow - Sponsored Content & Brand Retainers

## Phase 1: Brief Audit & Ethical Fit Analysis (Day 1–3)
1. Ingest brand brief via `brand_brief_and_ethical_fit_analyzer.ts`.
2. Ensure minimum 70/100 ethical score and zero deceptive marketing claims.
3. Execute `SPONSORED_EDUCATIONAL_CONTENT_AGREEMENT.md` with selected retainer tier ($500 / $1,500 / $3,000).

## Phase 2: Educational Tutorial & Case Study Creation (Day 4–8)
1. Synthesize hands-on educational case study integrating sponsor SDK or developer tool.
2. Inject FTC § 5 and ASCI statutory disclosure badges.
3. Submit draft to sponsor for factual verification (5-day window).

## Phase 3: Edge Publishing & Campaign Attribution
1. Publish sponsored tutorial with rel="sponsored nofollow" link enforcement.
2. Ingest lead and download attribution events via Edge Sponsor Gateway.
3. Provide sponsor with sub-10ms real-time dashboard token.

## Phase 4: Monthly Executive Review & Invoicing
1. Issue monthly retainer invoice via `invoicing_engine.ts` on the 1st of each month.
2. Deliver verified ROI and audience completion rate telemetry report.
