# Ethical Brand Outreach & Sponsorship Playbook
**Framework:** Technical Value-First Brand Partnerships  
**Target:** 5 Recurring Enterprise Retainers ($15,000/mo Sponsorship MRR)

---

## Phase 1: Identifying Ethical Developer & HealthTech Brands (Days 1–15)
1. **Target Companies:** Cloud infrastructure providers, API-first healthtech platforms, developer observability tools.
2. **The Ethical Filter:** Run `brand_brief_and_ethical_fit_analyzer.ts` before initial contact. Reject brands with deceptive consumer health or financial claims.
3. **The High-Density Hook:** Pitch a specific technical solution your audience struggles with that the sponsor solves (e.g., "How our audience can drop clinical FHIR latencies to 8ms with your SDK").

---

## Phase 2: Transparent FTC/ASCI Positioning (Days 16–30)
1. Present strict editorial independence as a selling point: audiences trust and engage with sponsored tutorials precisely because they are transparent and technically honest.
2. Provide the sub-10ms edge attribution snippet for seamless lead tracking.

---

## Phase 3: Renewal & Retainer Conversion (Days 31–60)
1. Share real-time telemetry from `audience_engagement_and_roi_telemetry_tracker.ts` showcasing engaged read-through rates.
2. Transition one-off campaigns to $3,000/mo Category Exclusive Retainers.
